# Code of conduct

IDEN follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not
the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull requests, or
anywhere else the project meets.

To report conduct that breaks it, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
**Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** reaches them alone), or by any private channel you
already share with them. Reports are kept confidential.
